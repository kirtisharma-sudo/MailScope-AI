#  MailScope AI

### AI-Powered Email Threat Detection, Geolocation & Forensic Intelligence Platform

> **Detect • Investigate • Correlate • Report**

MailScope AI is a **privacy-first cybersecurity platform** designed to analyze suspicious emails and turn them into structured, evidence-backed forensic intelligence.

Instead of stopping at a simple **“Safe / Malicious”** classification, MailScope analyzes the email's content, headers, authentication results, URLs, attachments, domains and available infrastructure indicators to help security teams understand **why an email is suspicious and what evidence can support further investigation**.

---

##  Problem Statement

Modern phishing and Business Email Compromise (BEC) attacks are becoming increasingly convincing.

Attackers may use:

- Spoofed sender identities
- Lookalike domains
- Social engineering and urgency
- Malicious URLs
- Credential-harvesting pages
- Suspicious attachments
- Compromised or relay infrastructure
- AI-generated phishing content

Traditional email security systems are mainly focused on **detecting or blocking threats**.

However, investigation often requires analysts to manually collect evidence from multiple sources.

### The gap

**Detection tells us that something may be wrong.  
Forensic intelligence helps us understand why and what to investigate next.**
